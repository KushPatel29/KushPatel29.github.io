# Challenge a decision and the controls behind it

These additions deepen existing projects. They preserve the distinction between
public project execution, real visitor analytics, paid experience and independent
human acceptance. No project score is a hiring probability.

| Question | Open the evidence | What to challenge |
|---|---|---|
| Is a faster query still the same answer? | [Control-tower SQL and Spark study](https://github.com/KushPatel29/supply-chain-control-tower/blob/master/benchmarks/QUERY_PERFORMANCE.md) | Independent integer totals, repeated timing samples, physical plans and the workload/layout boundary. The executed Spark joins use a separate bounded local[2] CI engine. |
| Does the policy satisfy both commercial priorities? | [S&OP challenge](https://github.com/KushPatel29/supply-chain-control-tower/blob/master/docs/SOP_DECISION_REVIEW.md) | The units-optimal policy reduces revenue-weighted fill. Inspect joint service floors and the fixed investment ceiling before choosing a candidate. |
| What happens to a correction older than seven days? | [Executed dbt recovery drill](https://github.com/KushPatel29/supply-chain-analytics-dbt/blob/master/docs/HISTORICAL_RECOVERY.md) | The ordinary increment misses it; bounded repair, replay and schema recovery must reconcile with a full rebuild. |
| Did a human accept this exact release? | [Migration UAT/release packet](https://github.com/KushPatel29/legacy-to-fabric-migration/blob/master/docs/business-analysis/MANUAL_UAT_EXECUTION.md) | Six manual scenarios remain pending. Stale-candidate reviews and critical defects block readiness. Local rollback is separate from Service publication. |
| Is the apparent experiment winner trustworthy? | [Interactive experiment decisions](../projects/portfolio-intelligence-platform/dist/experiment.html) | Sample size, allocation mismatch, uncertainty and error guardrails. All examples are synthetic; the page collects nothing. |
| Can higher EBITDA hide a cash problem? | [Budget challenge memo](https://github.com/KushPatel29/excel-fpa-model/blob/main/budget_cycle/challenge_output/DECISION_MEMO.md) | Price elasticity assumptions, interest, working capital and peak debt across four balanced statement scenarios. |
| Can a bad model release be refused and rolled back? | [Wildfire release/decision monitor](https://github.com/KushPatel29/wildfire-prediction-app/blob/main/docs/RELEASE_RECOVERY.md) | Actual alias APIs in a disposable registry copy, validation-only gates, artifact integrity and prospective lead slices. The live forecast is preserved. |
| Is an alert explanation ready for review? | [AML investigation quality](https://github.com/KushPatel29/aml-transaction-monitoring/blob/main/docs/INVESTIGATION_QUALITY_REVIEW.md) | The source evidence, lawful alternative, unresolved questions and proposed next step. Structural completeness cannot authorize closure or a filing. |

## What still needs external evidence

An independent practitioner review and genuine manual UAT need actual people.
Licensing and tenant access are needed for Service publication and actual
Power Platform execution. Professional AML adjudication, enterprise on-call
ownership, real business-partner budget cycles and credentials cannot be obtained
by relabelling a personal project. New prospective outcomes need time.

Use the [reviewer guide](reviewer-guide.md) to reproduce a flagship calculation
and provide specific feedback. Feedback and approval remain pending until given.
