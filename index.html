The method

My favorite results are the losses.

Anyone can show a model that wins. Showing the model that lost — and shipping the boring one that beat it — is what production judgment looks like.

84.9% > 80.0%

The ranker lost

A two-stage retrieval + gradient-boosted ranker lost to plain collaborative filtering on a customer-disjoint holdout. The simple model ships; CI enforces that the winner keeps winning.

18.5% < 19.6%

The forecaster lost

A gradient-boosted, direct multi-step forecaster lost to a moving average on WAPE. The moving average ships, and the challenger's loss is documented in the README.

p ≈ 0.23

The alarm that didn't cry wolf

A fairness screen fired on the attrition model. Fisher's exact test showed small-sample noise, not bias — so it became a monitor with a CI gate, not a build-breaker.

Every repo also documents what I deliberately didn't build — no vector database where SQL is the right tool, no deep learning on 38 SKUs, no cloud cosplay. Knowing when a technique would be decoration is the actual skill.
